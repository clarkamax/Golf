# Enterprise Electric Inc. — Business Handbook (SAMPLE)

## About us
Enterprise Electric Inc. is a small residential and light-commercial electrical
contracting business. We handle repairs, installations, panel upgrades, lighting,
and electrical safety work.

## Hours
- Office hours: Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM (local time).
- We are closed weekends and major holidays.
- After-hours and weekend calls are for **emergencies only** (see below).

## Services we provide
- Outlet, switch, and fixture repair/replacement
- Circuit breaker and electrical panel work / panel upgrades
- Lighting installation (indoor, outdoor, recessed)
- Ceiling fan installation
- EV charger installation
- Troubleshooting flickering lights, dead outlets, tripping breakers
- Electrical safety inspections

We do **not** do: appliance repair, HVAC, plumbing, or generator servicing.

## Emergency vs. routine (IMPORTANT for classification)
Treat a call as **URGENT / emergency** if the caller describes any of:
- Sparking, burning smell, or smoke from outlets/panels/wiring
- Exposed or downed wires; a wire arcing
- Complete power loss to a home (not a neighborhood-wide outage)
- Water in contact with electrical equipment/panels
- Any situation the caller says is dangerous or a shock/fire risk

Treat as **routine / appointment** if it is a normal repair, installation, quote
request, or scheduling question with no immediate safety risk.

## Pricing (SAMPLE — do not quote as final)
- Standard service-call/diagnostic fee: around $95 (waived if work is booked).
- Detailed pricing requires an on-site assessment.
- Do **not** commit to exact prices on the phone — say a technician will confirm.

## Team (SAMPLE)
- Owner / lead electrician: "Sam" (receives urgent alerts).
- Two field electricians and one office coordinator.

## Tone & style
- Friendly, calm, professional, and concise. Speak plainly — this is a phone call.
- Reassure callers with emergencies and make clear help is being arranged.
- Never promise a live transfer or an exact callback time. Say the team will call
  the caller back — as soon as possible for emergencies, or during business hours
  for routine matters.
